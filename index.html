The Three Segments

Every delegation competes in all three — Brand Brush, Brain Child, and the Ad Challenge.

01

Brand Brush

Each team receives an existing, well-known brand. Teams create a themed stall that presents its products or services. Judges visit each stall and ask probing questions during a live Q&A.

02

Brain Child

Teams pitch an original business idea through a 5–7 slide presentation. The proposal should explain the business objective, product or service insight, pricing with reasons, and future development and promotion plans. Judges assess quality, feasibility, and sustainability.

03

Ad Challenge

Teams produce a 30–90 second video advertisement for the business developed in Brain Child. Submit the video at least one week before the event. Judges evaluate marketing, media, and IT skills. The top three advertisements will be displayed, and the best will be showcased at the award ceremony.
